Add the Download button to your browser

One time only. Takes about 30 seconds.
No Chrome, Edge or Brave found. Install Google Chrome, then come back to this page. (Safari and Firefox can't use this extension; the Grabbit website works in any browser.)
  1. Open the Extensions page

    Click the button, then click your browser's address bar, paste (Ctrl+V) and press Enter.

    The address is chrome://extensions. You can also find Extensions in the browser's menu.

  2. Turn on Developer mode

    The switch is at the top right of the page.

  3. Click Load unpacked and choose Grabbit Extension

    First click Put it on my Desktop below.

    The button shows up near the top of the page once Developer mode is on. In the window that opens, click Applications in the sidebar, select Grabbit Extension, then click Select.

  4. Drag the Grabbit Extension folder onto the Extensions page

    Or click Load unpacked and choose that folder.
